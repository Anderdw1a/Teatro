## What is ORTZAI?

**Ortzai** is an independent theatre laboratory and performing arts school based in **Vitoria-Gasteiz** (Basque Country, Spain). Founded by director and pedagogue **Iker Ortiz de Zarate**, the company has become a cultural landmark due to its commitment to **bilingual theatre production in Basque and Spanish**.

### Our Value Proposition

1.  **Bilingual Theatre:** We offer performances in both official languages of the Basque Country, promoting local culture and linguistic accessibility.
2.  **Professional Training:** A theatre school for all ages, from youth initiation to advanced adult classes.
3.  **Classics and Avant-garde:** Our repertoire ranges from contemporary takes on Lorca and Shakespeare to current playwrights.

### Location and Contact
Ortzai operates primarily from its headquarters in Vitoria-Gasteiz, actively contributing to the city's cultural agenda with annual premieres and intensive workshops.
